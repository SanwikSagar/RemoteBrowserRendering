STEP 3: Deploy on Render.com:
        1. Go to https://dashboard.render.com
        2. Sign up with GitHub (free)
        3. Click "New +" → "Web Service"
        4. Select your "RemoteBrowserRendering" repo
        5. Click "Create Web Service"
        6. Wait 5-10 minutes ☕

🔧 KEEP IT AWAKE 24/7:

Render's free tier sleeps after 15 min of inactivity.

Solution: Use UptimeRobot (free)
1. Go to https://uptimerobot.com
2. Create a monitor
3. URL: Your Render URL
4. Check interval: Every 5 minutes
5. Done! Your browser stays awake 24/7

🆘 TROUBLESHOOTING:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

"Service Unavailable"
→ Wait 30 seconds (cold start after sleep)

"WebSocket connection failed"
→ Check backend is fully deployed (green checkmark)

Slow performance
→ Lower FPS to 30 in settings
→ Reduce quality to 70%

Build failed on Render
→ Check build logs in Render dashboard
→ Ensure all files are committed
